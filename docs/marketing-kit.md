# GalaTayo marketing kit

Ready-to-post copy for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Every fact below comes from a GalaTayo place page (which credits its own sources) and is cross-checked against the source named in brackets. Prices are GalaTayo's "from ₱X per head" starting budgets and can change, so captions say "from" or "around".

Rules for anyone posting:

- Real places, real facts. If a fact is not on the place page or in a named source, leave it out.
- One link per post, to the exact place or guide page (never just the home page).
- Taglish is welcome, but every caption should still make sense to a foreign tourist.
- One or two exclamation points per caption, max. No "hidden gem", "nestled", "breathtaking", "must-visit", "unforgettable".
- Credit photos the way the place page does (author, licence, source). Use only our own photos or properly licensed ones.

## 1. What people actually search (from the keyword engine)

Use these exact phrasings in hooks, on-screen text and first lines. Source: `frontend/scripts/seo/data/keyword-map.md` (Google and Bing autocomplete, YouTube suggestions, Google Trends, Wikipedia pageviews).

- **"pasyalan sa ___"** and **"___ tourist spots itinerary"** beat "things to do" for many Pinoy searchers. Say both: "Pasyalan sa Manila (aka things to do in Manila)".
- **"date ideas"**, **"date spots manila"**, **"things to do in manila for couples"**: date content is the biggest topic with no city attached.
- **"with kids"** / **"for family"**: "things to do in tagaytay with kids", "bohol itinerary with kids", "baguio itinerary with kids".
- **"where to eat in ___ at night"** and **"___ food trip"**: Baguio, Tagaytay, Makati and Cebu lead.
- **"___ vlog low budget"** and **"___ vlog 2026"** on YouTube: budget-first, current-year videos win.
- Place questions people type: **"entrance fee"**, **"how to get there"**, **"opening hours"**, **"best time to visit"**. Answer one of these in the first 3 seconds of a video.

### Metro Manila

**1. Fort Santiago (TikTok/IG Reel)**
Rizal walked his last steps here, and you can literally follow them.
Fort Santiago in Intramuros has bronze footprints tracing Jose Rizal's final walk out of the fort in 1896. The Spanish started building it in 1571, and the river view from the Baluarte de Santa Barbara still hits. Entry from around ₱75.
Plan it: galatayo.app/places/manila/fort-santiago
#FortSantiago #Intramuros #ThingsToDoInManila #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Fort Santiago"._

**2. Binondo (TikTok)**
The world's oldest Chinatown is a food crawl, not a photo op.
Binondo was founded in 1594, across the river from Intramuros. Pick 4 or 5 stops on Ongpin instead of queueing everywhere, bring small bills, go on a weekday morning. Free to walk, just budget for the food.
Food crawl map: galatayo.app/places/manila/binondo-chinatown
#Binondo #ManilaFoodTrip #FoodTrip #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Binondo"._

**3. National Museum of Fine Arts (IG carousel)**
Free entry, aircon, and a painting almost 8 meters wide. Sulit na date.
Juan Luna's Spoliarium (1884) is over 4 meters tall and almost 8 meters wide, and it's in the National Museum of Fine Arts beside Rizal Park. Pick a few halls, take it slow, come on a weekday morning.
galatayo.app/places/manila/national-museum-of-fine-arts
#NationalMuseum #Spoliarium #FreeThingsToDo #DateIdeasManila
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Spoliarium"._

**4. Jones Bridge (Reel, blue hour)**
Paris energy over the Pasig, ₱0.
Jones Bridge was inspired by Paris's Pont Alexandre III and got its old-world lamps and La Madre Filipina statues back in 2019. Quick photo stop at blue hour, stay on the sidewalk, then walk to Binondo for dinner.
galatayo.app/places/manila/jones-bridge
#JonesBridge #Manila #PhotoSpot #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Jones Bridge"._

**5. Intramuros (FB post)**
One walled city, three anchors, zero rushing.
Intramuros was founded in 1571, and San Agustin Church inside it is a UNESCO World Heritage Site that survived World War II. Our tip: museums in the morning, a slow walk on the walls at sunset.
Full plan: galatayo.app/places/manila/intramuros
#Intramuros #PasyalanSaManila #HeritagePH
_Source: GalaTayo place page; UNESCO, "Baroque Churches of the Philippines"._

**6. Rizal Park (TikTok)**
Free family gala that comes with a dancing fountain.
Luneta covers 58 hectares by Manila Bay. See the Rizal Monument and its honor guards, stand on Kilometer Zero, then stay for the musical fountain in the evening. Libre lahat.
galatayo.app/places/manila/rizal-park-luneta-park
#Luneta #RizalPark #FreeThingsToDoManila #FamilyGala
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Rizal Park"._

**7. Manila Ocean Park (Reel)**
Walking under sharks without getting wet? Possible sa Luneta.
The Oceanarium is a 25-meter, 220-degree glass tunnel with sharks and rays swimming over you. Time it with the 12:30 PM or 4:30 PM shows and go on a weekday morning. Tickets from around ₱720.
galatayo.app/places/manila/manila-ocean-park
#ManilaOceanPark #RainyDayManila #FamilyGala #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Manila Ocean Park official site._

**8. Art in Island (TikTok, rainy-day hook)**
Bawal ang "do not touch" dito. Rainy-day plan, solved.
Art in Island in Cubao is a 3,800-square-meter 3D museum where you pose inside the murals. Bring a barkada willing to take turns on the camera and a fully charged phone. From around ₱850.
More indoor plans: galatayo.app/guides/indoor-activities-in-metro-manila
#ArtInIsland #RainyDayIdeas #IndoorActivities #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Art in Island official site._

**9. The Mind Museum (FB, parents)**
Science pero super fun, and it's award-winning.
The Mind Museum in BGC has more than 250 interactive exhibits across five galleries and won a THEA Award, a top global prize for themed attractions, for its exhibits. Book online, go on a weekday morning before school groups.
galatayo.app/places/taguig/the-mind-museum
#MindMuseum #ThingsToDoWithKids #BGC #FamilyGala
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "The Mind Museum" (THEA Award)._

**10. Ayala Triangle Gardens (IG, Christmas season)**
Makati's runway era: this park used to be an airport.
Ayala Triangle Gardens sits on the old Nielson Field, Manila's prewar airport, and from November to January the Festival of Lights turns the trees into a free light-and-music show. Late afternoon is the sweet spot.
galatayo.app/places/makati/ayala-triangle-gardens
#AyalaTriangle #FestivalOfLights #ChristmasInManila #DateIdeasManila
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Ayala Land, "Ayala Triangle Gardens"._

**11. Poblacion (TikTok, Thursday post)**
Thursday na, alam mo na. Poblacion bar crawl, the walkable way.
Start with dinner, then hop from a chill bar to a louder one along Kalayaan, Polaris, Durban and Enriquez. Thursday to Saturday nights are the most buhay. Budget from around ₱500 a head.
galatayo.app/places/makati/poblacion-makati
#Poblacion #MakatiNightlife #BarkadaNight #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page "Poblacion Makati (Bar District)"._

**12. Toyo Eatery (IG, anniversary date)**
Lutong bahay flavors, one Michelin star.
Chef Jordy Navarra's Toyo Eatery in Makati uses only Philippine ingredients and ferments in-house, so toyo, suka and patis taste like home in a whole new way. Multi-course dinner, reserve ahead. From around ₱2,500 a head.
galatayo.app/places/makati/toyo-eatery
#ToyoEatery #MichelinManila #DateNightManila #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; MICHELIN Guide Philippines._

**13. Cubao Expo (Reel)**
QC's creative hideout used to be a shoe expo.
Cubao Expo was built in the 1970s as the Marikina Shoe Expo and is now vintage shops, record stores, galleries and bars. Browse in the late afternoon, stay for dinner and drinks.
galatayo.app/places/quezon-city/cubao-expo
#CubaoExpo #ThingsToDoInQC #PasyalanQC #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Araneta City" (Cubao Expo)._

### Day trips and Luzon

**14. Burnham Park, Baguio (TikTok, fresh news hook)**
Burnham Lake is back, and so are the swan boats.
Baguio's Burnham Park lake reopened on September 1, 2026 after a ₱100-million rehabilitation, with boating back. Free to enter, a short walk from Session Road. Go on a weekday morning for calm water.
Baguio plan: galatayo.app/guides/things-to-do-in-baguio
#Baguio #BurnhamPark #BaguioItinerary #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page "Burnham Park" (Baguio City announcements)._

**15. Café by the Ruins, Baguio (IG)**
The Baguio breakfast regulars won't skip.
Café by the Ruins opened in 1988, started by Baguio artists and writers including National Artist BenCab. Order Ernie's camote bread, bagnet and a cup of Ruins Coffee on a cold morning. From around ₱400.
galatayo.app/places/baguio/cafe-by-the-ruins-baguio
#CafeByTheRuins #BaguioFoodTrip #WhereToEatInBaguio
_Source: GalaTayo place page._

**16. Baguio Night Market (TikTok)**
Ukay hunters, 9 PM sa Harrison Road.
Every night the road beside Burnham Park closes to cars and fills with thrifted jackets, bags and shoes, plus cheap street food. Come right at opening for first dibs. Budget from around ₱100.
galatayo.app/places/baguio/baguio-night-market
#BaguioNightMarket #Ukay #BaguioLowBudget #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page._

**17. Kiltepan, Sagada (Reel)**
Alarm at 4 AM, reward at sunrise.
Kiltepan is Sagada's most famous sunrise spot. On lucky mornings a sea of clouds fills the valley. Clouds are never guaranteed, the mountains are. Bring a jacket, go on a weekday.
galatayo.app/guides/things-to-do-in-sagada
#Sagada #SeaOfClouds #Kiltepan #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page._

**18. Halo-Halo de Iloko + Ma-Cho Temple, La Union (IG carousel)**
Fried halo-halo, then a temple with a sea view.
In San Fernando, Halo-Halo de Iloko serves halo-halo wrapped in lumpia and fried, inside an ancestral house full of antiques. Walk it off at Ma-Cho Temple, a seven-storey Taoist temple built in 1975 that looks out over the West Philippine Sea. Halo-halo from around ₱150, the temple is free.
galatayo.app/places/san-fernando-la-union/halo-halo-de-iloko-san-fernando-la-union
#Elyu #LaUnion #HaloHalo #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place pages "Halo-Halo de Iloko", "Ma-Cho Temple"._

**19. Tangadan Falls, La Union (TikTok)**
Pahinga muna sa beach, Elyu barkada.
Tangadan Falls in San Gabriel is a two-tier falls with deep pools, a short guided hike from the road. Easy half-day side trip from San Juan. Bring a dry bag. From around ₱200.
galatayo.app/places/san-gabriel/tangadan-falls-la-union
#TangadanFalls #LaUnion #ElyuItinerary
_Source: GalaTayo place page._

**20. Calle Crisologo, Vigan (Reel)**
Parang time travel, and it's free.
Vigan is a UNESCO World Heritage city, and Calle Crisologo is its cobblestone street of bahay na bato houses. Walk it early for empty-street shots, come back at night for the lamps, then empanada at Plaza Burgos.
galatayo.app/places/vigan/calle-crisologo-vigan
#Vigan #CalleCrisologo #IlocosTrip #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; UNESCO, "Historic Town of Vigan"._

**21. Hundred Islands (FB, barkada)**
123 islands, one boat, split by five.
Hundred Islands in Alaminos is the Philippines' first national park, with 123 islands at high tide. Boats leave from Lucap Wharf. Rent a full-day boat as a group of five to split the cost, and climb the Governor's Island viewdeck. From around ₱400.
galatayo.app/places/alaminos/hundred-islands-national-park-alaminos
#HundredIslands #Pangasinan #IslandHopping #BarkadaTrip
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Hundred Islands National Park"._

**22. Masungi Georeserve (IG)**
Adult jungle gym, reservation only.
Masungi in Baras, Rizal has rope bridges, hanging walkways and the giant Sapot net over limestone karst. Small guided groups, and every visit helps fund reforestation. Book an early dry-season slot.
galatayo.app/places/baras/masungi-georeserve-rizal
#Masungi #DayTripFromManila #Rizal #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Masungi Georeserve official site._

**23. Sky Ranch, Tagaytay (TikTok, family)**
Ferris wheel with a Taal view. Sunset ride, then dinner sa ridge.
Sky Ranch's Sky Eye is a 63-meter Ferris wheel overlooking Taal Lake and Taal Volcano. Go late afternoon on a weekday for short lines. Rides from around ₱100.
galatayo.app/places/tagaytay/sky-ranch-tagaytay
#SkyRanch #Tagaytay #ThingsToDoInTagaytayWithKids

### Visayas and Mindanao

**24. Chocolate Hills, Bohol (Reel)**
At least 1,260 hills, and they really do turn brown.
The Chocolate Hills cover more than 50 square kilometers of Bohol, and the grass turns chocolate-brown in the dry season. Go early or late in the day for soft light. Entry from around ₱100.
Bohol plan: galatayo.app/guides/things-to-do-in-bohol
#ChocolateHills #Bohol #BoholItinerary #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Chocolate Hills"._

**25. Tarsier Sanctuary, Bohol (FB, responsible travel)**
Fist-sized, giant-eyed, and very easily stressed. Visit the right way.
At the Philippine Tarsier Foundation sanctuary in Corella, tarsiers stay in natural forest. Talk softly, no flash, no touching, and skip the roadside cages. From around ₱100.
galatayo.app/places/corella/philippine-tarsier-sanctuary-corella
#Tarsier #Bohol #ResponsibleTravel #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Philippine Tarsier Foundation._

**26. Kawasan Falls, Cebu (TikTok)**
That blue is real. Bamboo raft under the falls, anyone?
Kawasan in Badian is a three-tier cascade fed by a mountain spring, and the grand finale of the Badian canyoneering trip. Arrive early in the dry season for clear water. From around ₱100.
galatayo.app/places/badian/kawasan-falls-badian
#KawasanFalls #Cebu #CebuItinerary #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page._

**27. Temple of Leah + Tops, Cebu (IG carousel)**
The "Taj Mahal of Cebu" is a love story. Pair it with the city view at Tops.
Teodorico Adarna built the Temple of Leah in the Busay hills for his late wife, Leah. Go late afternoon for golden light, then up to Tops Lookout for the night view. From around ₱100 each.
Cebu plan: galatayo.app/guides/things-to-do-in-cebu-city
#TempleOfLeah #CebuCity #DateIdeasCebu #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place pages "Temple of Leah", "Tops Lookout"._

**28. Manokan Country + Calea, Bacolod (TikTok food trip)**
Bacolod in two stops: inasal, then cake.
At Manokan Country, pour the chicken oil over your rice the Negrense way. Then line up at Calea for the white chocolate cheesecake. Both from around ₱150.
galatayo.app/places/bacolod/manokan-country-bacolod
#Bacolod #Inasal #FoodTrip #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place pages "Manokan Country", "Calea Pastries and Coffee"._

**29. Boracay Sunset Paraw Sailing (Reel)**
The most Boracay thing to do at golden hour.
A paraw is a traditional double-outrigger sailboat. You sit on the net while it glides along White Beach, 30 minutes to an hour, and the boatmen take your photos. Book the last half hour before sunset. From around ₱500.
Boracay plan: galatayo.app/guides/things-to-do-in-boracay
#Boracay #ParawSailing #BoracaySunset #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page._

**30. Sumilon Island, Cebu (IG)**
The sandbar that changes shape with the season.
Sumilon off Oslob was declared the Philippines' first marine protected area in 1974, so the reef is worth a snorkel. Most people pair it with a morning in Oslob. From around ₱500.
galatayo.app/places/oslob/sumilon-island-sandbar
#Sumilon #Oslob #CebuSouth #GalaTayo
_Source: GalaTayo place page; Wikipedia, "Oslob" (Sumilon Island section)._
