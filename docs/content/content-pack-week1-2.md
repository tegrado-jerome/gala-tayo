# GalaTayo content pack: weeks 1–2 (7–20 Oct 2026)

14 daily short-form posts for TikTok, IG Reels and FB (Reels or carousel), plus 5 trend templates and alternative hooks for the top 3.
This pack is about formats. The general caption bank lives in `docs/marketing-kit.md`, so nothing here repeats it.

## Ground rules for this pack

- **Every place is live and visible** on galatayo.app (checked against Supabase `places` with `status = active`, minus the `hidden` list in `frontend/src/data/galaWorthy.json`, on 6 Oct 2026).
- **Every price comes from the place's own budget note.** If a note says "around", the post says "around". Fares, flights and stays are never included unless the post says so.
- **Every CTA URL returned HTTP 200** on 6 Oct 2026. Fake paths on the site return a real 404, so a 200 is a real page.
- **Photos** come from `frontend/src/data/placeGalleryPhotos.json`. Photo `#N` of a place is
  `https://media.galatayo.app/places/<slug>/hd/<slug>-N.webp`.
  Put the credit line on screen (small, bottom corner) **and** in the caption. For CC BY-SA photos you crop or zoom, add "(cropped)".
- **App screen recordings:** record the real app on a test plan. Never mock up a screen or fake an AI answer. If the real output differs from the script, change the script to match.
- **Tone:** mostly English with light Taglish (tara, sulit, barkada). No politics, no religion jokes, no tragedy, no jokes about real people. Churches, shrines and memorial sites only appear as respectful background, never as a punchline.
- **Posting times** are best guesses from common PH scrolling habits: the lunch break, the after-work and after-dinner scroll, and slow weekend mornings. Check the analytics after week 1 and move slots that underperform.

## Day 1 · Wed 7 Oct · POV: the GC that never decides

**Format:** POV skit + screen recording, 20–25 s, 9:16
**Place:** Hundred Islands National Park, Alaminos (Pangasinan)
**App features:** swipe deck (TARA! / PASS), date poll, Hatian

**Hook (on screen, 0–2 s):** "POV: the barkada GC has said 'tara!' 47 times and still no date 💀"

**Script**
1. (0–2 s) Phone screen scrolling a GC full of "tara!", "g ako!", "kelan?", "kayo bahala". Hook text on top.
2. (2–5 s) Creator stares at the camera. Text: "kaya ako na."
3. (5–10 s) Screen recording: the GalaTayo swipe deck, swiping one place left (PASS) and Hundred Islands right (the TARA! stamp shows). Text: "Step 1: everyone swipes. No more 'kayo bahala'."
4. (10–14 s) Screen recording: date poll with the "Best date" result. Text: "Step 2: vote the date. Majority wins."
5. (14–19 s) Photo of Hundred Islands with plain maths on screen:
   "Full-day boat (1–5 pax): ₱1,920 ÷ 5 = ₱384
   + registration ₱120 each
   = around ₱504 each (before fare)"
6. (19–23 s) Screen recording: the Hatian tab, someone marked "Settled". Text: "Step 3: Hatian shows who still owes the host. Wala nang singilan sa GC."
7. (23–25 s) End card: "One link. Tara na talaga. galatayo.app"

**Shot list**
- GC screen: a fake chat made with the team's own test accounts. No real names or faces of non-consenting people.
- Swipe deck, poll and Hatian: real screen recording of a test plan with Hundred Islands in it.

- Hundred Islands photo #2 as an extra B-roll cut. Credit: "Photo: Cid Jacobo / CC BY-SA 4.0"

**Caption**
Every GC has that one trip that's been "next month" since 2024 😭 Ditch the endless "kayo bahala". Swipe the spots, vote the date, and let Hatian track who's settled. Hundred Islands with 5 friends works out to around ₱504 each (₱1,920 full-day boat split 5 ways + ₱120 registration, fare not included). Tag the friend who always says "g ako" then ghosts 👀
📍 galatayo.app/places/alaminos/hundred-islands-national-park-alaminos
Photos: Robert Anton Pimentel Aparente, Cid Jacobo (CC BY-SA 4.0)

**Hashtags:** #barkadatrip #HundredIslands #Pangasinan #galatayo

**CTA:** https://galatayo.app/places/alaminos/hundred-islands-national-park-alaminos

**Hook strength: 9/10.** Almost every Filipino barkada has lived this exact GC. The 💀 and the oddly specific "47 times" read as real life, not an ad. The ₱504 figure is a concrete payoff that makes people save and share it.

## 5 safe trend-riding templates

Trends were checked on 6 Oct 2026 using public, no-login sources only: Google Trends daily RSS (PH), Google News RSS, Google Autocomplete and the r/phtravel public RSS.

| # | Template | Trend signal (source) | How to use it | Why it's safe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"Things that cost ₱0 in ___"** | Budget "is it worth it?" threads, e.g. "Eyeing Batanes is it worth it" and "How do you balance traveling while saving money?" in r/phtravel top posts this month | Hook: "___ 'mahal'? These cost ₱0 to enter." 4–5 places with `budget_min = 0`, then an honest card for the real costs | Uses only our own budget data and shows the real costs too |
| 2 | **Micro-trip / 1-night reset** | "Micro-trip" and "micro-getaway" travel-trend coverage (Parade, Jul 2026; News18, 4 Oct 2026) | "1 night, 0 signal, ₱___" with Anawangin or Nagsasa Cove camping (from around ₱700 / ₱900, boat per group) or Taal Heritage Town | A lifestyle trend with no person, brand or tragedy attached |
| 3 | **Seat-sale "Day 1 sorted"** | Domestic seat-sale stories this week (Philstar Life, 1 Oct; AirAsia Newsroom "10.10" sale, 5–11 Oct) | "Booked the promo fare? Here's Day 1 when you land." 3 stops near the airport town (e.g. Basco Lighthouse is about 1.2 km uphill from Basco Port) | Never name an airline or quote a fare; we only add what to do on arrival |
| 4 | **Long-weekend countdown** | 3-day weekend from 31 Oct to 2 Nov 2026 (Cebu Daily News, 23 Sep 2026); "long weekend october/november 2026" in autocomplete | "__ days to the 3-day weekend. Pick your vibe: beach / bundok / heritage" with one place each, CTA to the weekend-getaways guide | Talk only about "the 3-day weekend". No Undas or cemetery jokes, and don't use the holiday's meaning as a hook |
| 5 | **Tier list / "barkada trip characters"** | Autocomplete: "tier list filipino food", "barkada trip characters" | Rank 5–6 real places (food, viewpoints, waterfalls) or cast 5 lovable characters, each solved by an app feature | Kind by design: everyone ends up S tier or lovable; no business gets dissed |

**Skipped on purpose:** the "airport theory" cut-it-close trend (unsafe advice), El Niño heat (trending, but over 350,000 people are affected, so it's not content), name-based meme trends about real people, and a viral interview clip that makes fun of a person.
